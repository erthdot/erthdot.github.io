Colour Psychology
Understanding what each colour communicates helps you make better brand decisions.
Blue
TRUST · CALM · AUTHORITY
Blue signals reliability and professionalism. It reduces anxiety and builds confidence — a staple for finance, healthcare, and technology brands.
Used by: FNB, Zanaco, Stanbic, Standard Chartered
Green
GROWTH · NATURE · HEALTH
Green connects to life, sustainability, and prosperity. Darker greens feel premium and trusted; brighter greens feel fresh and organic.
Used by: MTN Money, Airtel Money, WhatsApp
Red
ENERGY · URGENCY · PASSION
Red commands attention and creates a sense of urgency. It's powerful for food, retail, and entertainment — but use carefully in professional services.
Used by: Airtel, Coca-Cola, YouTube, Netflix
Orange & Gold
WARMTH · CONFIDENCE · SUCCESS
Orange radiates friendliness and enthusiasm. Gold signals prestige and achievement. Both are great for brands wanting to feel accessible yet aspirational.
Used by: MTN, Zambia National Commercial Bank
Purple
LUXURY · WISDOM · CREATIVITY
Purple has historically been associated with royalty and sophistication. It works beautifully for creative agencies, premium services, and spiritual organisations.
Used by: Hallmark, Cadbury, FedEx
Black
ELEGANCE · POWER · AUTHORITY
Black is timeless and powerful. It conveys sophistication, exclusivity, and seriousness. Ideal for luxury goods, legal services, and high-end fashion.
Used by: Chanel, Apple (dark mode), Rolls-Royce
Yellow
OPTIMISM · CLARITY · ATTENTION
Yellow is the first colour the eye sees. It sparks optimism and mental clarity — perfect for children's education, retail promotions, and brands that want to feel approachable.
Used by: MTN, Zambia Sugar, DHL
Teal & Cyan
BALANCE · CLARITY · INNOVATION
Teal blends the trust of blue with the growth of green. It feels modern and balanced — popular with healthcare brands, tech startups, and wellness companies.
Used by: Health clinics, fintech apps, NGOs
